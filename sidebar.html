New in — paper and ink

The whole app wears the landing page's paper now: warm off-white, ink type, hairline rules, and Fraunces on the titles. The header is a 20px mark and the wordmark, and the tagline is gone. Everything you could do before still works exactly the same way.

  • The swarm builds your resume. Generate a resume or a cover letter and a swarm of agent cursors rebuilds it block by block as a paper sheet — selection boxes, rulers, the lot. It takes about eight seconds, there is a Skip button, and Settings → Interface turns it off.
  • Ink theme. Settings → Interface → Ink theme, or the toggle in any header, flips the paper to a dark ground for night work. Your PDFs are untouched either way.
  • Colour means something. Ink fill is the main action. Green confirms, red destroys, orange is the plan, blue is information. Nothing decorative.
