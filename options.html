Interface Build on

Ink theme (dark paper)

Paper is the default warm off-white. Ink flips the same hues to a dark ground for night work. Everything else stays identical. The build simulation is the swarm of agent cursors that rebuilds your resume as a paper sheet after each generation. Turn it off to save instantly with no simulation. Everything else — the theme, the extractions, the PDFs — is unaffected.

Review extracted job details

Before each PDF is saved, PocketResume shows the job title, company, location and recipient it read from the posting so you can correct them. Typing in that box stops the 30-second auto-save.

Also download the PDF

Generated PDFs are kept in the side panel so you can drag them straight onto a page. Turn this on to also save a copy to your computer.

Write it here

Writes a short general letter from this resume. Contact details are redacted. Pollinations runs first, local Ollama is the fallback. One letter per minute keeps the free provider available.

Or write it with an assistant (optional)

  1. Open ChatGPT, Claude, or any assistant you already use.
  2. Click Copy prompt and paste it in.
